Comprehensive Hydraulic Analysis & Optimization System

Modern water distribution network design and analysis

Survey, mapping, hydraulic analysis, pipe sizing, quantities and drawings, all in one model. What you design is what gets priced and built.

Download FlowCHAOS Get the User Manual

Version 1.0.0 for Windows 10 or later. What's new. Freeware, no licence fee.

One workflow

From the survey to the tender drawings, everything works from one model, so a change made once shows up everywhere.

Survey

Bring in survey points, drawings, shapefiles, imagery and elevation models.

Draw

Draw pipes to exact lengths and bearings, with snapping and full editing.

Analyse

Simulate pressures, flows and water quality over a day or a moment.

Design

Size pipes at least cost, check fire flow and cut long sections.

Deliver

Produce quantities, programmes and print-ready drawing sheets.

Advanced hydraulic analysis

Run steady-state and extended-period simulations on complex distribution networks, with pumps, valves, tanks, controls and water quality.

  • Steady-state and extended-period runs
  • Water age, chlorine and source tracing
  • Several solution methods to compare

Networks from CAD drawings

Open a DXF drawing or a shapefile and turn its lines into a network you can analyse. FlowCHAOS joins the pipes, reads their sizes and points out anything that needs fixing.

  • Drawings open with their layers, colours and texts
  • Pipes joined where lines meet or cross
  • Gaps and overlaps found and fixed

Precise drawing and editing

Type lengths and bearings, snap to what is already there, and edit with move, copy, trim, extend and split. Every change can be undone.

  • Typed lengths, angles and coordinates
  • Trim, extend, split and reshape pipes
  • Copy properties from one element to many

A GIS made for water

Work over satellite imagery and street maps, bring in shapefiles and drawings, and use over forty spatial tools without leaving your model.

  • Basemaps in any coordinate system
  • Spatial tools, selections and a field calculator
  • Search and query any layer

Terrain and surfaces

Take ground levels from elevation models, online terrain or your survey, then draw contours, relief maps, 3D views and long sections.

  • Smooth contours from survey points
  • Long sections with cover and the grade line
  • Elevation models checked against survey control

Who uses FlowCHAOS

From a village supply to a regional scheme.

Water utilities

Find low-pressure areas, test pipe failures and plan extensions on current imagery.

Consulting engineers

Design and size schemes, with quantities and programmes that follow the design.

Irrigation designers

Design sprinkler, drip and pivot systems, from crop water needs to field layout.

Universities and colleges

Teach with calculations students can open, check and understand.

Free and easy to start

No licence fee and no keys. Install FlowCHAOS, open a sample project and start modelling in minutes, whether it is your first network or a city's.
