User guide

Analysis options

Every option of Analysis > Analysis Options: what it does, its usual value and when to change it.

Analysis > Analysis Options has a tab for each subject. The options are kept with the project and written into the hydraulic model file when the network is exported. Point at any field for a short explanation.

Hydraulics

Units and friction

Option Choices or range Default Meaning
Flow units L/s, L/min, ML/day, m3/hour, m3/day, US gal/min, ft3/s, US Mgal/day, Imperial Mgal/day, acre-ft/day L/s units of flow in the project and its exchange files; metric flow units keep the other quantities metric
Head loss formula Hazen-Williams, Darcy-Weisbach, Chezy-Manning Hazen-Williams the friction law; each pipe keeps a roughness for each formula, so switching back and forth loses nothing
Specific gravity 0.01 to 100 1.0 density of the fluid relative to water at 4 degrees C
Relative viscosity 0.001 to 1000 1.0 kinematic viscosity relative to water at 20 degrees C; used by Darcy-Weisbach

Demand

Option Default Meaning
Demand model Demand-driven Demand-driven delivers every withdrawal whatever the pressure; pressure-driven delivers what the pressure allows
Demand multiplier 1.0 scales every withdrawal of the run: 1.8 for a peak hour, 0.4 for night flow
Default demand profile 1 the profile of withdrawals that name none
Emitter exponent 0.5 the exponent n of emitter outflow q = C p^n (0.5 for orifices, sprinklers and most leaks; 0.5 to 1.5 for leaks in plastic pipe)

Pressure-driven withdrawal

Used when the demand model is pressure-driven.

Option Default Meaning
Minimum pressure 0 m at or below this pressure nothing is delivered
Required pressure 0.1 m at or above this pressure the full withdrawal is delivered; set it from what the outlet serves (a yard tap 5 to 10 m, a house connection 15 to 20 m, a sprinkler 25 to 35 m)
Pressure exponent 0.5 the shape of the delivery curve between the two pressures

Convergence

Option Default Meaning
Maximum trials 40 iterations allowed for one hydraulic state
Accuracy 0.001 the state has converged when the sum of the flow changes, divided by the total flow, is below this
Maximum head error 0 (not checked) largest error allowed in the head loss of any link, in metres
Maximum flow change 0 (not checked) largest flow change allowed in any link at convergence
Status check frequency 2 iterations between checks of pump, valve and check valve status
Status checks up to trial 10 after this trial, status is checked only at convergence
Damping limit 0 (none) below this accuracy, flow corrections are halved to settle hard networks
If a state does not converge Stop the run stop the extended period, or continue with a warning
Status report Changes of status what the solver reports: status changes, nothing, or every trial (for diagnosing a network that will not balance)

Raise Maximum trials to 200 for large or difficult networks. A network that does not converge is usually a modelling problem (a closed loop of valves, a pump that cannot lift, disconnected nodes): Analysis > Solver Warnings names the elements involved.

Water Quality

Option Default Meaning
Parameter None what extended-period runs follow: chlorine, a chemical, water age or source tracing
Traced source the node whose water is traced
Quality tolerance 0.01 the smallest difference of quality that keeps two parcels of water apart
Relative diffusivity 1 molecular diffusivity relative to chlorine in water; 0 means wall reactions are not limited by mass transfer

For a full study use Analysis > Water Quality Analysis (Water quality).

Reactions

Option Default Meaning
Bulk reaction order 1 order of the reaction in the water
Wall reaction order 1 0 or 1
Tank reaction order 1 order of the reaction in tanks
Global bulk coefficient 0 bulk rate of every pipe without its own (1/day, negative for decay)
Global wall coefficient 0 wall rate of every pipe without its own (m/day for first order)
Limiting potential 0 (none) a concentration a reaction tends to and stops at
Roughness correlation 0 (none) makes wall rates follow pipe roughness, so older pipes react more

Times

Durations and steps are written as H:MM or H:MM:SS.

Option Default Meaning
Simulation duration 0:00 the length of an extended period (0 is a steady state)
Hydraulic timestep 1:00 the step between hydraulic solutions; shorter steps follow tanks and controls more closely
Quality timestep 0:05 the step of the quality transport; shorter is more accurate
Profile timestep 1:00 the length of one period of the demand profiles
Profile start 0:00 the period of the profiles at the start of the run
Report timestep 1:00 how often results are kept
Report start 0:00 the time from which results are kept
Rule timestep 0:00:10 how often rules are checked between hydraulic steps
Start clock time 12 am the time of day when the run starts; clock-time controls and rules count from it

Energy

Option Default Meaning
Global pump efficiency 75 % the efficiency of pumps without an efficiency curve
Global energy price 0 price of a kWh for pumps without their own tariff
Energy price profile multipliers of the price through the day (a night tariff); a pump can have its own in its properties
Demand charge 0 a charge per kW of the highest pumping power

Analysis > Energy Report gives, for each pump, the share of the time it runs, its mean flow, head and efficiency, the energy per cubic metre pumped, its mean and peak power, and its energy and cost per day, with the totals, the peak power of all pumps together and the demand charge. In an extended period the energy is summed over every time step, with the efficiency at each step's flow (from the pump's efficiency curve) and the price at that time of day.

ID Labels and Element Defaults

ID Labels sets the prefix, the first number and the step of the names of new elements (J1, J2; P1, P2 and so on). Element Defaults gives new junctions their design offset and withdrawal, new tanks their size and levels, and new pipes and valves their diameter, roughness and material, so a network can be drawn quickly and corrected afterwards.
